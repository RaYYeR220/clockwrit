The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development announces that the Founding Day of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia for this year, which is on Sunday, 5 Ramadan 1447 AH, corresponding to 22 February 2026, is an official holiday for the public, private and non-profit sectors.
