HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the
Legislative Assembly of Alberta, enacts as follows:

Definition

1 In this Act, "official time" means the time 6 hours behind
Coordinated Universal Time.

RSA 2000 cD-5 s1;2026 c12 s3

Time observed

2 No person shall use or observe within Alberta any time other
than official time.

RSA 2000 cD-5 s2;2026 c12 s3

Reference to time

3 A reference to time in an enactment, order, rule, proclamation,
bylaw, resolution, agreement or other document is, unless
otherwise expressed, a reference to official time.

2026 c12 s3

Municipalities and Metis settlements bound

4(1) No municipality or Metis settlement shall adopt, proclaim,
enforce, use or observe any time other than official time.

(2) A bylaw passed or proclamation issued by the council or an
official of a municipality or Metis settlement providing for the
adoption, proclamation or enforcement of the use or observance of
any time other than official time is void.

Regulations

5 The Lieutenant Governor in Council may make regulations

(a) prescribing a term other than "official time" by which
official time may be known;

(b) defining any term used but not defined in this Act;

(c) providing for any transitional matters arising under this Act;

(d) respecting any matter or thing the Lieutenant Governor in
Council considers necessary or advisable to carry out the
purposes of this Act.

RSA 2000 cD-5 s5;2026 c12 s3

Consequential amendments to regulations

6(1) For the purposes of making any necessary changes as a result
of this Act, the Lieutenant Governor in Council may, by regulation,
amend any regulation filed under the Regulations Act.

(2) A regulation authorized by this section may be made
notwithstanding that the regulation being amended was made by a
member of the Executive Council or some other person or body.
