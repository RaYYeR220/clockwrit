Translation:

Second (KEDUA): The dates of 1 Ramadan 1447 H, Idul Fitri 1447 H and Idul Adha 1447 H are fixed by Decree of the Minister of Religious Affairs. Set in Jakarta on 19 September 2025.
ANNEX - Joint Decree of the Minister of Religious Affairs, the Minister of Manpower and the Minister of State Apparatus Empowerment and Bureaucratic Reform Nos. 1497, 2 and 5 of 2025 on National Holidays and Collective Leave 2026.
A. NATIONAL HOLIDAYS 2026: 1 Jan (Thu) New Year 2026; 16 Jan (Fri) Isra Mi'raj of the Prophet Muhammad; 17 Feb (Tue) Chinese New Year 2577 Kongzili; 19 Mar (Thu) Nyepi (Saka New Year 1948); 21-22 Mar (Sat-Sun) Idul Fitri 1447 H; 3 Apr (Fri) Good Friday; 5 Apr (Sun) Easter; 1 May (Fri) International Labour Day; 14 May (Thu) Ascension of Jesus Christ; 27 May (Wed) Idul Adha 1447 H; 31 May (Sun) Vesak 2570 BE; 1 Jun (Mon) Pancasila Day; 16 Jun (Tue) Islamic New Year 1448 H (1 Muharram); 17 Aug (Mon) Independence Proclamation; 25 Aug (Tue) Mawlid of the Prophet Muhammad; 25 Dec (Fri) Christmas.
B. COLLECTIVE LEAVE (CUTI BERSAMA) 2026: 16 Feb (Mon) Chinese New Year; 18 Mar (Wed) Nyepi; 20, 23 and 24 Mar (Fri, Mon, Tue) Idul Fitri 1447 H; 15 May (Fri) Ascension; 28 May (Thu) Idul Adha 1447 H; 24 Dec (Thu) Christmas.
