Daylight saving time: Egypt to set clocks forward by 60 mins on April 25th
According to the Daylight Saving Time Law, daylight
saving time in Egypt will be implemented on the last Friday of April, which
this year falls on April 25, 2025. Daylight saving time will return, with
clocks set forward 60 minutes, starting from the last Friday of April until the
last Thursday of October.
The adoption of daylight saving time in Egypt came
nearly seven years after its abolition. It was implemented on the last Friday
of April 2023. According to Cabinet statements, daylight saving time involves
setting the clock forward 60 minutes, while winter time is set back 60 minutes.
Friday was chosen to adjust both daylight saving and
winter time, as Friday is an official holiday in most government sectors. This
was chosen to avoid any time-related problems or errors, and to give citizens
an opportunity to understand and implement the change, avoiding any disruption
to government and institutional operations if the change occurs on a normal
workday.
