Encerra a hora de verão no território nacional.

art. 84, caput , inciso IV, da Constituição, e tendo em vista o
disposto no art. 1º, caput , inciso I, alínea “b”, e § 2º, do
Decreto-Lei nº 4.295, de 13 de maio de 1942,

DECREE No. 9,772 OF 25 APRIL 2019
Ends daylight saving time in the national territory.
THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC, using the power conferred on him by art. 84, caput, item IV, of the Constitution, and in view of art. 1, caput, item I, letter "b", and § 2, of Decree-Law No. 4,295 of 13 May 1942,
DECREES:
Art. 1 Daylight saving time is ended in the national territory.
